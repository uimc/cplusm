Are the ceremony and reception at the same place?

Yes! All wedding day events will be held at The Cordelle in Nashville, TN.

What should I wear?

We ask that guests dress in colorful, funky cocktail attire. By funky, we mean fun colors, textures or prints, or just something that makes you feel great!

Do I get a plus one?

We want to see all of our favorite people at the wedding and keep it a personal event! If you have a plus one, it will be indicated on your invitation and you will be prompted to fill in their name and dietary information when you RSVP for the event.

Are any of the wedding events outdoors?

Yes, the ceremony will take place in the yard of the venue! The cocktail hour will be held on the porch of the venue.

Where will I park?

There will be street parking around the venue, but there is no designated parking. As the event will begin during working hours on a Friday, we recommend carpooling or taking a ride share to the event if you are able! There are also several hotels within walking distance of the venue if you are looking to get some steps in.

Will there be vegetarian options?

Yes! There will be a vegetarian entree available that you can select when you RSVP to the event. Please let us know any and all dietary restrictions when prompted so that they can be relayed onto our catering team.
