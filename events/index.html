Our Wedding Day

Below outlines the schedule for our big day!

All events below will take place at The Cordelle in Nashville, Tennessee on Friday, October 2nd, 2026.

Wedding Ceremony

5:00pm - 5:30pm

Our wedding ceremony will begin at 4:30 pm. Please arrive 15-30 minutes prior to the start time to ensure that you are seated before it begins.

Weather permitting, the ceremony will take place in the yard at The Cordelle. Please wear shoes that you are comfortable walking (and dancing) in!

Cocktail Hour

5:30pm - 6:30pm

Our cocktail hour will take place on the porch of The Cordelle following the ceremony. There will be hors d’oeuvres and drinks available! The bar will serve wine, beer, and signature cocktails.

Reception

6:30pm - 11:00pm

The wedding reception will take place inside The Cordelle. You will let us know your meal selection and any dietary restrictions when you RSVP for the event. Dinner will be followed by dessert and lots of dancing!
